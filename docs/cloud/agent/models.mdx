## BU 4.0 models

Browser Use's own models share one adjustable reasoning setting:
`modelParams.reasoning.effort` accepts `low`, `medium`, `high`, or `xhigh`,
with `high` by default.

- **BU 4.0 Mini** (`bu-4.0-mini`) is fast and low cost, available in the US and EU.
- **BU 4.0** (`bu-4.0`) offers the best balance and is the new US project default.
- **BU 4.0 Pro** (`bu-4.0-pro`) is the most capable.

BU 4.0 and BU 4.0 Pro are US-only for now. All three use standard processing
only, with no fast or priority service tiers. They always run on Browser Use's
platform route; a project's own provider keys are not used for them.
BU 4.0 and BU 4.0 Mini are available on the free plan; BU 4.0 Pro needs credits.

Each model ID stays pinned with no silent upgrades. Future generations use
versioned IDs such as `bu-5.0`, `bu-5.0-pro`, and `bu-5.0-mini`.

show a smaller curated set; availability can also depend on your account's
rollout and configured provider key. An accepted ID does not guarantee access
to every upstream provider. Experimental models are not recommended as a
production default.

  MiniMax M3 (`minimax-m3`) is currently unavailable. Runs and follow-up
  tasks that request it are refused at creation with 403
  `Model minimax-m3 is unavailable: this deployment has no MiniMax route configured.`
  Choose another model until it returns.

## Bring your own key

BYOK is available to pay-as-you-go customers; no recurring subscription is required. After adding credits, add an Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google key under **Settings →
API Keys → Bring Your Own Key**. V4 uses it automatically for matching models;
no request flag is needed. You pay the provider directly, plus a 0.2× Browser
Use orchestration fee. Models from other providers use Browser Use-managed
keys.

BU 4.0 models always use Browser Use's platform route, even when the project
has its own provider keys.
